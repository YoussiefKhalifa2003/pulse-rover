# Pulse-Rover — LinkedIn kit

## Headline

Pulse-Rover MagDock: a browser micro-rover that boards your hand and rides with you

## Hook

Open your palm. The rover evaluates the landing pad, hesitates, Maglocks on, and rides when you lift your hand — then disembarks to finish its mission. All in the browser. No markers.

## What people remember

The robot trusts you enough to climb onto your hand.

## Tech bullets

- MagDock Boarding Protocol: evaluate → hesitate → soft/hard dock → boarded → airborne → disembark
- Dual-hand roles: flat palm = mobile base; other hand paints
- Mission pause/resume while boarded; secured cargo rides the hand stack
- Canvas 2D + MediaPipe Hand Landmarker (local, no install)
- Optional Instant Replay from telemetry (press R) — MagDock is the hero

## Demo script (25–35s)

1. Open flat palm — pad + confidence  
2. Hesitation + Maglock engaged  
3. Lift hand — rover rides  
4. Set down near core — disembark / secure  
5. Quiet DELIVERED
